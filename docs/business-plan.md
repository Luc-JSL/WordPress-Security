# Business plan: Secure WP Care

*As of October 27, 2026. Figures marked "estimate" are judgment calls, not measured data.*

## The decision

After comparing dozens of low-phone side businesses, **WordPress security and care plans** won
because it is the only option that:

1. Needs **no sales calls** (support calls are fine; selling is by email and marketplaces).
2. **Recurs monthly**, and gets more profitable per hour as it grows (one dashboard runs many sites).
3. **Matches the work Jordan enjoys** (securing and fixing websites) and the cybersecurity degree.
4. Costs about **$0–100 to start**.
5. Lets Claude do much of the work (tools, reports, drafting), with Jordan as the accountable expert.

Runner-up: car-niche YouTube video editing (fun, but income grows only with hours, and AI tools
are pushing prices down).

## Who needs it

| Fact | Number |
|---|---|
| WordPress share of all websites | about 40% (W3Techs, Oct 2026, via secondary reports) |
| Business WordPress sites with a detectable vulnerability | 80% (Singapore study, 102 sites, 2026) |
| Sites with at least one vulnerable plugin or theme | 42% (Patchstack, 2021) |
| New WordPress vulnerabilities in 2025 | 11,334, up 42%; 91% in plugins; 46% unpatched at disclosure (Patchstack) |
| Small businesses reporting an attack or incident per year | roughly 40–60% (Hiscox, UK government, Devolutions) |

**Estimate:** about 65% of small businesses on WordPress need this; under 1% are searching in any
month, so the business goes to them with a free health check.

## Pricing

| Plan | Price |
|---|---|
| Agency white-label | $30–40 per site/month |
| Essential | $79/month |
| Secure | $99/month |
| Hacked site cleanup | $150–300 one-time |
| Email Lockdown add-on (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) | $150–300 one-time |

Market reference: white-label providers charge agencies $39–79 per site; agencies resell at $100–250.

## Sales channels (no calls)

1. **Free website health check** emailed to local businesses, 30 a week (main channel).
2. **White-label partnerships** with small web agencies and freelance designers.
3. **Hacked-site rescue** listings on Upwork and Fiverr, converted to monthly plans.
4. **Local network:** Conway Chamber of Commerce, Facebook groups, family and friends.
5. **Referrals:** one free month per signed referral.

## Money and odds (estimates)

| Month | Sites | Monthly income |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | 2 pilots | $100–150 |
| 4 | about 8 | $500–600 |
| 6 | about 18 | $1,100–1,300 |
| 8–9 | about 30 | about $2,000+ |
| 12 | 40–50 | $3,000–3,500 |

| Milestone | Chance if the plan is followed |
|---|---|
| First paying client within 60 days | about 65–75% |
| $2,000/month by month 9 | about 35–45% |
| $2,000/month by month 12–15 | about 50–60% |

Nothing is guaranteed; consistency (30 emails a week) is the biggest lever.

## Next tools to build

1. Plugin version detection + Wordfence Intelligence vulnerability data (free for commercial use).
2. Google Web Risk check (is Google warning visitors about the site?), free up to 100,000 lookups/month.
3. Technology detection with enthec/webappanalyzer.
4. Full email-security validation with checkdmarc.
